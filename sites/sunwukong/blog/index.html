Name and Meaning (Etymology)

The name means "Monkey awakened to emptiness" and reflects the figure's role in the tradition. Sūn Wùkōng, the Monkey King, is the rebellious protagonist of Journey to the West. Born from a stone egg on the Mountain of Flowers and Fruit, he learns daoist arts, acquires his iron staff from the Dragon King, and wreaks havoc in heaven before being imprisoned under Five Elements Mountain by the Buddha. His later conversion and service to the monk Xuánzàng frame the epic pilgrimage, transforming unchecked power into disciplined spiritual energy.
