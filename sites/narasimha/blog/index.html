Name and Meaning (Etymology)

The name means "The Man-Lion — the fourth avatāra of Viṣṇu" and reflects the figure's role in the tradition. Narasiṃha is the man-lion avatāra of Viṣṇu who destroys the demon Hiraṇyakaśipu. Appearing from a pillar at dusk, he uses claws rather than weapons to circumvent the demon's boons of invulnerability. The myth celebrates divine ingenuity and the protection of the devout child Prahlāda. The image of Narasiṃha bursting from the pillar became a favorite iconographic subject, representing the omnipresence of divine protection in places where violence is forbidden.
