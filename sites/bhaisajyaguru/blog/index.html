Name and Meaning (Etymology)

The name means "Medicine master" and reflects the figure's role in the tradition. Bhaiṣajyaguru, the Medicine Buddha, presides over the pure land of Vaiḍūryanirbhāsa. He vowed to heal the physical and mental afflictions of beings and is invoked in healing rituals across Mahāyāna and Vajrayāna Buddhism. His deep-blue color and medicine-bowl emblem identify him in iconography. The twelve great vows of Bhaiṣajyaguru include the promise to cure disease, relieve poverty, and guide beings toward awakening through medical and spiritual means.
