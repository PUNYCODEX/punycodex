Name and Meaning (Etymology)

The name means "Lord who looks down in compassion" and reflects the figure's role in the tradition. Avalokiteśvara is the bodhisattva of compassion who hears the cries of all sentient beings. In Mahāyāna sutras he descends into every realm to save the suffering, manifesting in forms appropriate to those he teaches. In East Asia he became the goddess Guānyīn, while in Tibet he is the patron bodhisattva manifesting through the Dalai Lamas. The Heart Sūtra and Lotus Sūtra celebrate his willingness to assume any form—male, female, monarch, or beggar—to liberate beings from suffering.
