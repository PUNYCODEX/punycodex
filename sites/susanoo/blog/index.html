Name and Meaning (Etymology)

The name means "Swift impetuous male deity; the Shinto storm god" and reflects the figure's role in the tradition. Susanō is the Shinto storm god, brother of Amaterasu and Tsukuyomi. The Kojiki and Nihon Shoki recount his quarrel with Amaterasu: after rampaging through her heavenly fields, she withdraws into a cave and plunges the world into darkness. Susanō later atones by slaying the eight-headed serpent Yamata-no-Orochi at Izumo and recovering the sword Kusanagi, one of the imperial regalia.
